Reveal the strategy ✧

Start at the end. Work backwards.

Finish your turns at 1 → 4 → 7 → 10. After the computer adds 1, add 2; after it adds 2, add 1. Together, the two moves always add 3.

The computer checks the possible continuations and chooses a winning move whenever one exists. Finding the outcome of every position is what it means to solve a game.
